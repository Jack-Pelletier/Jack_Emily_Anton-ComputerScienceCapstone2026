About Capstone Street

What is Capstone Street?

Capstone Street is a platform designed to make it easier for students, faculty, and outside organizations to discover and share student capstone projects. The website provides a central location where students can showcase the work they have completed and where visitors can explore projects based on their interests.

Capstone projects represent the knowledge, skills, and experience that students have developed throughout their education. Capstone Street gives these projects a place where they can remain accessible beyond the classroom and can be discovered by other students, faculty members, and organizations interested in student work.

Our Purpose

The purpose of Capstone Street is to improve the accessibility and visibility of student projects. Users can browse and search through projects to discover work related to different areas of computer science, data science, and technology.

By organizing projects in one accessible location, Capstone Street helps students learn from previous projects while also providing opportunities for their own work to reach a wider audience.

Our Values

Accessibility

Student work should be easy to find and explore. Capstone Street is designed to provide a simple way for users to discover projects and learn more about them.

Collaboration

Sharing projects encourages students, faculty, and organizations to exchange ideas and discover opportunities to work together.

Student Achievement

Capstone Street gives students a place to showcase the work, knowledge, and skills they have developed throughout their education.

Privacy

Capstone Street values the privacy of students who share their work. Providing personal or contact information is completely optional, allowing students to showcase their projects without being required to share information they would prefer to keep private.
